Full-Resolution Recovery

Upgrades feed thumbnails to original 1920x1080 (and portrait 1080x1920) CDN masters while excluding small profile avatars.

Embedded EXIF Datestamps

Writes each post's original capture date into JPEG APP1 EXIF tags (DateTimeOriginal) in local memory so Apple Photos and Google Photos sort chronologically.

100% Local & Private

Zero external servers, zero analytics, and zero telemetry. Media downloads directly from your authenticated session to your Downloads/Nursery_Photos/ folder.
